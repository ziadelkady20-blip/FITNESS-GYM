# FITNESS GYM — UI/UX direction

The final site should feel like a premium fitness brand, not a generic gym template.

## Visual hierarchy
1. Cinematic hero with strong typography and restrained motion.
2. Real gym photography as the visual anchor once supplied.
3. Memberships as an interactive comparison experience.
4. Short, high-impact content blocks with generous spacing.
5. Strong red accent used selectively; black remains the canvas and white carries hierarchy.

## Motion principles
- Use motion to explain hierarchy and state, not as decoration everywhere.
- Prefer smooth reveal, parallax, magnetic/hover feedback and section transitions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep mobile interactions touch-friendly and avoid hover-only information.

## Asset strategy
The official logo and real gym photography will be supplied later. Layouts intentionally reserve space for them instead of inventing final imagery.
